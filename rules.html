Tutorial

How to play

Every round opens the same way: two beats to sync up, then everyone moves at once.

BEAT 1
Clap
BEAT 2
Clap
BEAT 3
Go

Slap your thighs (or clap) twice in sync with your opponent — that's the timing window. On the third beat, both players commit to a move simultaneously. No takebacks, no re-reads.

The Core Three

Charge

Cost: none · Gain: +1 charge

Cross your arms and reload. Free to use, but you generate zero defense — get hit while charging and you're eliminated on the spot.

Shield

Cost: none · Blocks: standard Boom

Stops a normal Boom cold, free of charge. Can't bank charges while shielding, and it does nothing against anything that pierces.

Boom

Cost: 1 charge · Blocked by: Shield

Point and fire. Eliminates anyone who isn't shielding. Both Boom at once and you both go down.

The Pop-Culture Upgrades

Double Boom

Cost: 2 charges

A heavier shot that pierces a standard Shield outright.

Ultimate

Cost: 3 charges

An unblockable finisher — wins the exchange outright unless it's answered by a Reflect.

Reflect

Cost: 1 charge

Sends any incoming attack — Boom, Double Boom, even an Ultimate — straight back at whoever fired it.

3 lives. Sudden reads. No draws.

  • Each fighter starts a round with 3 lives
  • Boom, Double Boom, Ultimate, and Reflect hits all remove one life
  • Trading identical attacks costs both players a life
  • Charges cap at 4
  • Best 2-of-3 rounds wins the match
