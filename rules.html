Read the room before the round starts

How to play

Every round opens the same way: two beats to sync up, then everyone moves at once. Read on for the core three, the advanced tech, and how a match is won.

The rhythm

Clap. Clap. Go.

Slap your thighs (or clap) twice in sync with your opponent — that's the timing window. On the third beat, both players commit to a move simultaneously. No takebacks, no re-reads: you're locked in the instant the beat hits.

The core three

Charge

Cost: none · Gain: +1 charge

Cross your arms and reload. Free to use, and the only way to earn ammo — but you generate zero defense while doing it. Get hit while charging and you're eliminated on the spot.

Shield

Cost: none · Blocks: standard Boom

Cross your forearms and brace. Stops a normal Boom cold, free of charge. The tradeoff: you can't bank charges while shielding, and it does nothing against anything that pierces.

Boom

Cost: 1 charge · Blocked by: Shield

Point and fire. Eliminates anyone who isn't shielding — including someone mid-Charge. If you both Boom at once, you both go down. It's a read, not a guarantee.

Advanced tech — banked charges only

The pop-culture upgrades

2 charges

Double Boom

A heavier shot that pierces a standard Shield outright. If your opponent's guarding, this is how you punish it.

3 charges

Ultimate (Hadouken / Kamehameha)

An unblockable finisher. It wins the exchange outright — unless it's answered by a Reflect.

1 charge

Reflect (Mirror)

A defensive gamble that sends any incoming attack — Boom, Double Boom, even an Ultimate — straight back at whoever fired it. Whiff it against a non-attack and the charge is wasted.

Winning a match

3 lives. Sudden reads. No draws.

  • Each fighter starts a match with 3 lives.
  • Any successful hit — Boom, Double Boom, Ultimate, or a Reflected attack — removes one life.
  • Trading identical attacks costs both players a life.
  • Charges cap at 4, so hoarding forever isn't free either.
  • First fighter to zero lives is eliminated — best 2-out-of-3 rounds takes the match.
